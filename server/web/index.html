Delta-hedge a sold call

A bank sells calls and holds Δ shares, rebalancing at each step along one stock path (Hull 19.4). The option is set by the inputs on the left: σ there is the implied vol the option was sold at, used for Δ. The stock itself moves at the realised vol below.

Stock path and shares held

Does hedging work? (Hull Table 19.1)

One path is luck. This hedges many simulated paths at each rebalancing frequency: the average cost should sit at the Black-Scholes value, and the spread should shrink as rebalancing gets more frequent.

Volatility trading: what if the stock moves more or less than priced?

The bank sells at the implied σ on the left and hedges with it, but the stock really moves at different realised vols. Calm stocks are cheap to hedge, wild ones expensive: the P&L depends on how much the stock moves, not which way.
